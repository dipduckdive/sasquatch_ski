Sasquatch Ski

Ski as far as you can. When the chaser wakes, don’t stop.

  • ⚡ Speed Up: banked (3 to start, max 5). Grab 5 for 🔥 Super!
  • ⭐ Invincible: smash trees, bounce him back
  • 💥 Zap: destroys the chaser
  • 👻 Invisible: he loses your trail
  • 🔺 Ramps: jump clear of trees
  • 🦝 Raccoon: found after 2,000 m, he fights a chaser for you

Arrows or A/D to steer, Space for a 0.9 s double-speed burst (or tap the ⚡ button), down to go straight. Turn fully sideways to stop, keep holding to sidestep. On touch, hold left or right of the skier.
